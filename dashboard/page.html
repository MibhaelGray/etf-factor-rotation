      How ideas are scored

      Each idea enters at the session's open and exits at its close. A pair is dollar-neutral, so its return is the long leg's minus the short leg's; an outright long or short is that one leg. A basket leg holds its 3 to 10 stocks in equal weight, so its return is their average. Returns are in basis points, where 100 bp is 1%.

      Final scores come from the nightly scoring run on daily bars. Until then, a session's ideas carry intraday marks from IBKR. The path through the day uses 15-minute bars from the 09:30 bar's open, so its end can differ slightly from the official score. The hit rate's range is a 95% Wilson interval, which treats ideas as independent; ideas on the same day often move together, so the true range is wider. Ideas are research, not advice, and carry no position sizing.
