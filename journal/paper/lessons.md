# Standing lessons

- Don't take a post-earnings gap (held or faded) on the screen's numbers alone; name what will carry the reaction forward, and check what the reaction was about (a deal, a split, departures) rather than the beat or miss. The mechanical 11-09 gap trades went 3 for 9, mean about -307 bp (ABNB, CVS, U, RDW, UBER and CGNX lost; OUST, QCOM and NVO won).
