## Independent review

The reviewer reproduced the base rates and kept the idea at confidence 2, saying standing aside is defensible. Findings:

- The headline flatters: the +133 bp mean is +92 bp without 2025-04-04 and +81 bp without April 2025. A realistic edge is +40-90 bp.
- None of the 11 cases had SPY above its 200-day. SPY down 2% or more, within 3% of or above it: 8 of 11, +44 bp.
- Closest fits: SPY down 3% or more after an up day, 7 of 8, +105 bp; SPY down 2% or more with SMH down 5% or more, 10 of 13, +88 bp (with the prior day up, 7 of 7, +99 bp); VIX up 30% or more, 6 of 8, +108 bp.
- Against: VIX 25-32, 9 of 17, +5 bp; spread down 2.5% or more since July, 9 of 19, -11 bp; 20-day spread at -15% or worse, 7 of 10, mean -10 bp.
- The 2026 SMH counter-analog is weaker than stated above: only one of the five had SPY down 2% or more (+106 bp), and its losers came with SPY -0.1% and -1.8% and VIX at 16-19.
- SMH/XLU is no better: +63 bp, 62% up; 2026 -182 bp, worst -642 bp.
- Stand-down rules changed: SMH replaces the SPHB/SPLV ratio, because SPLV printed nothing in the 09:15 bar on Nov 5 and SPHB only 2,100 shares. SPY moved to 715, since 720 is only about 0.85 of VIX1D's implied move and would have cancelled +225 and +541 bp cases. The SPHB level rule was dropped, the yield test tightened to 4.92%, and headlines now need a named vehicle and a dollar size.
- The credit, Vanda and "already cut" claims were overstated: HYG is the only Nov 5 credit reading, retail bought TQQQ and SOXL while selling single stocks, and gross leverage is still about 310%.
